Item 1.03 Bankruptcy or Receivership.

On October 19, 2026, Acme Corporate Holdings Inc (the "Company") and its primary operating subsidiaries filed voluntary petitions under Chapter 11 of Title 11 of the United States Code in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York.
